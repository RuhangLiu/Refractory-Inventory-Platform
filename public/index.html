Inventory calculations

Available stock
Current quantity minus reserved quantity.
Inventory value
Current quantity multiplied by unit price.
Action required
Available quantity is below warehouse safety stock.
Suggested order
150% of safety stock minus available and in-transit quantities; minimum zero.
